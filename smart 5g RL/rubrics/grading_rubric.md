# Grading Rubric

## Total: 100 points

## A. Problem Understanding (15)

- Correct explanation of 5G classes and QoS: 10
- Clear motivation and scope boundaries: 5

## B. Simulator Quality (20)

- Correct environment dynamics and interfaces: 10
- Clean modular implementation and documentation: 10

## C. Baselines (15)

- At least 3 baselines implemented correctly: 10
- Fair and consistent comparison protocol: 5

## D. RL Formulation and Method (20)

- Well-justified state/action/reward design: 10
- Sound RL implementation and training strategy: 10

## E. Evaluation and Analysis (20)

- Comprehensive metrics beyond reward: 10
- Ablation, robustness, and interpretation quality: 10

## F. Reproducibility and Communication (10)

- Reproducible repository and scripts: 5
- Clear report and final presentation: 5

## Bonus (up to +10)

- Strong research extension with convincing evidence
- Paper reproduction/adaptation with critical discussion

## Penalties

- Missing baseline comparison: up to -20
- Missing reproducibility instructions: up to -10
- Unsupported claims without evidence: up to -10
