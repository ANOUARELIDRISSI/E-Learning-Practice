# Research Questions

## RQ1 - Allocation Strategy

How should radio resources be allocated among eMBB, URLLC and mMTC users under limited PRBs?

## RQ2 - RL Formulation

How can the allocation problem be represented as an MDP with meaningful state, action and transition dynamics?

## RQ3 - Reward Design

Which reward formulation best captures operator objectives while enforcing QoS behavior?

## RQ4 - Algorithm Selection

Which RL algorithm is most suitable for the chosen action space and constraints?

## RQ5 - Comparative Performance

Does RL outperform classical resource-allocation baselines under identical simulation settings?

## RQ6 - Generalization

Does the learned policy remain effective under traffic pattern shifts?

## RQ7 - Robustness

How does performance degrade when channel conditions deteriorate?

## RQ8 - Practicality

What is the trade-off between performance gain and deployment complexity (training cost, inference time, reproducibility)?
